### 1. MongoDB
- What is MongoDB?
mongoDB is the database where we can store our information in the form of document.

- What is a collection?
A group of related document.

- What is a document?
every single individual record is known as document.

### 2. Mongoose
- What is Mongoose?
A library that helps Node.js work with MongoDB.

- What is a schema?
Rules defining the structure of a document.

- What is a model?
An interface used to work with a MongoDB collection.
